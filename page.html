Imperial Dunes of Dubai

5 Days / 4 Nights Package

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Upon arrival at Dubai International Airport, guests are warmly welcomed and transferred privately to City Avenue Hotel Muteena. As you drive through the city, Dubai immediately introduces itself through its dramatic skyline, modern highways, and vibrant cosmopolitan atmosphere. After check-in, the remainder of the day is at leisure, allowing guests to relax after their journey or begin exploring the nearby neighborhoods at their own pace.

The evening offers an ideal opportunity to experience Dubai’s energetic nightlife, local cafés, shopping streets, or simply unwind at the hotel while preparing for the adventures ahead.

Accommodation: City Avenue Hotel Muteena.Day 2 – Discovering Dubai’s Heritage & Modern Icons | Dubai City Tour + Dubai Frame

The morning begins with breakfast at the hotel before departing for a half-day guided Dubai City Tour. This immersive introduction to the city reveals the fascinating contrast between old and new Dubai. Guests explore the historical districts where traditional wind-tower architecture reflects the city’s trading past, while modern landmarks showcase Dubai’s transformation into a global luxury destination.

During the tour, travelers experience vibrant souks, scenic city avenues, cultural neighborhoods, and panoramic photo stops at some of Dubai’s iconic attractions. The journey continues with a visit to the spectacular Dubai Frame, one of the city’s most symbolic architectural landmarks. Standing between Old Dubai and New Dubai, the Frame offers breathtaking elevated views that visually connect the city’s past, present, and future in one extraordinary experience.

After the tour, guests return to the hotel for relaxation and leisure time. Overnight stay at City Avenue Hotel Muteena.

Places to Visit

Al Fahidi Historical District

Explore the cultural heart of Dubai at Al Fahidi Historical District. Dating back to the 19th century, this area is filled with restored wind-towered houses, narrow winding alleys, and cultural centers. The wind towers, or Barjeel, were an ingenious early form of air conditioning. Here, travelers can explore museums that reveal Dubai's evolution from a small fishing and trading town to a global hub.

Traditional Souks

Experience Dubai's bustling traditional markets where old trading traditions continue. Browse vibrant spice markets, textile vendors, and artisan shops that reflect Dubai's historic role as a trading port. The souks offer a sensory journey through authentic Emirati commerce, with narrow alleyways filled with exotic fragrances, colorful fabrics, and traditional crafts.

Dubai Frame

Visit the iconic 150-meter tall, picture-frame-shaped structure that literally frames old and new Dubai. From the sky deck, see the modest neighborhoods of old Dubai on one side and the futuristic skyline on the other. It's a powerful visual representation of the city's rapid transformation over decades, offering a reflective, educational, and photo-worthy experience.

Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo

Discover one of the world's most impressive indoor marine attractions located within Dubai Mall. Journey through mesmerizing underwater tunnels surrounded by sharks, rays, and thousands of marine species while learning about aquatic ecosystems from around the world. The Underwater Zoo further enhances the experience through interactive exhibits featuring exotic marine and rainforest creatures.

Downtown Dubai

Explore Dubai's vibrant core where business, leisure, and spectacle converge. Walking through the boulevards, visitors encounter luxury malls like Dubai Mall, artistic installations, and fountains synchronized to music. Downtown Dubai embodies the city's ambition and lifestyle, offering a perfect blend of shopping, dining, and entertainment.

Dubai Creek & Dinner Cruise

Enjoy an enchanting evening aboard a traditional dhow sailing along historic Dubai Creek. As the boat glides gently across these historic waters, guests witness the city illuminated against the night sky, where old trading districts blend beautifully with modern architecture. Experience an international buffet dinner with soft music and a cool evening breeze.

Desert Safari & Arabian Camp

Experience an afternoon-to-evening adventure into the golden dunes beyond the city. The safari includes thrilling dune bashing across soft sands, creating an exhilarating off-road adventure through the heart of the desert. At the desert camp, guests are welcomed into an authentic Arabian-style setting with cultural experiences, traditional entertainment, and a delicious BBQ dinner under the stars.

Burj Khalifa (Optional)

Ascend to the observation floor of the world's tallest building for breathtaking 360-degree panoramic views of Dubai, the desert, and the Persian Gulf. Standing at 828 meters, the Burj Khalifa was designed as an engineering marvel. From the observation deck, you can see the city's layout, man-made islands, sprawling neighborhoods, and architectural innovations.

Museum of the Future (Optional)

Experience cutting-edge architecture and innovative exhibits at Dubai's Museum of the Future. This iconic structure showcases the latest advancements in technology, design, and human innovation. Explore interactive displays and futuristic concepts that inspire imagination and reveal possibilities for tomorrow's world.

Palm Jumeirah (Optional)

Visit Dubai's most famous man-made landmark, the iconic Palm Jumeirah. This stunning palm-shaped island features luxury resorts, upscale dining, water sports, and pristine beaches. It's an engineering marvel and a symbol of Dubai's ambition and luxury lifestyle, offering stunning views and world-class amenities.
